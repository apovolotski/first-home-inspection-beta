Closed beta for first-time buyers

First Home Inspection guides safe, permitted photo capture during a home viewing and creates a report from your evidence and observations. This beta is for invited evaluation. It cannot establish that a home is safe, diagnose hidden water damage, confirm an active leak, or replace a professional home inspection.

Use the beta

Add a home using a nickname, choose a short viewing plan, and save an overview and clear close-up where requested. Use even lighting and keep the entire mark boundary and nearby surface in frame. A focus area is optional; the original photo stays intact.

Manual reports work without a model download. For experimental suggestions, open About, then Photo analysis, download the approximately 1.80 GB model on Wi-Fi, and enable local analysis. This preview requires a physical iPhone with at least 6 GB of memory; iPhone 15 is the reference beta device. The app supports iOS 18 and later.

Saving photos does not start the model. Tap Check saved photos only where suggestions would help. One check runs at a time with a 30-second break afterward. Keep the app visible during a check. Review suggestions against the original photo before keeping or dismissing them; unreviewed suggestions stay out of report findings.

If a check cannot finish

A warm phone, interrupted check, missing model or uncertain output leaves the area unassessed. Keep photographing and use your notes or a manual report. Let the phone cool before requesting another check. No paid service is called when local analysis cannot complete.

An empty result is not a pass. Ask about unexplained marks and repair history, and seek appropriate professional assessment. Do not touch, scrape, open equipment or enter unsafe areas to obtain a photo.

Send useful beta feedback

Use TestFlight feedback or email apov@me.com. Include the app version and build shown in About, iPhone model, iOS version, the steps you took, and what happened. Note whether a check was requested, whether the phone was charging, and whether it felt warm. Please redact personal details from any screenshots you choose to attach.

No message is sent from the app automatically. Do not send API keys, passwords, home addresses, seller documents or photographs of people. For an immediate hazard, leave the area safely and contact local emergency services or the relevant utility; do not wait for app feedback.
