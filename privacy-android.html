Android privacy policy

Alexander Povolotskiy · Updated November 5, 2026

Photos and reports stay on your phone

First Home Inspection helps buyers record a property viewing with a checklist, selected photos, notes, attributed answers and buyer-reviewed observations. There is no account, payment, advertising or cloud photo analysis. Your photos, selected focus areas, notes, answers, AI suggestions, review decisions and report snapshots are kept in the app's private local storage. The publisher cannot read them unless you deliberately share a report or support message.

Experimental photo analysis uses Google's Gemini Nano through Android AICore on supported devices. Photos and prompts are processed on the device, with no paid API or cloud inference fallback. Other phones retain the manual checklist and report workflow. Availability varies by model, software and region.

Camera and selected photos

You choose to open your camera app or the Android system photo picker. The app receives only the photo you capture or select. It does not request unrestricted photo-library access, contacts, precise location, microphone or advertising identifiers. Original selected image files may contain camera metadata, including a location saved by your camera; such metadata remains in the private evidence file. Avoid photographing people, private documents or details you do not want in your viewing record.

Google SDK diagnostics and model downloads

The Android app includes Google's ML Kit GenAI SDK. Google states that these SDKs collect device information (manufacturer, model, operating system, build and available ML accelerators), app package and version, device/user or other identifiers and per-installation identifiers, performance and error information, API configuration such as image format and resolution, input and output sizes, feature versions, events such as initialization or model downloads, and configured languages. These are used for diagnostics and usage analytics. This collection is different from sending photo content to a cloud AI service. ML Kit states that collected data is encrypted in transit and not transferred to third parties.

SDK diagnostics may occur when the SDK initializes or is used. The app does not offer an SDK diagnostic opt-out and the publisher does not receive a user analytics dashboard or sell this information. Google controls its service retention under its privacy policy. Contact Google for requests concerning data held by its services.

If you choose to prepare a downloadable local model, AICore may contact Google and download substantial model files using your network connection. Network services receive ordinary request information, such as an IP address. Use Wi-Fi. Model availability and downloads are managed by the Google system service.

Sources: ML Kit Android data disclosure, ML Kit on-device GenAI, and Google privacy policy.

You control report sharing

A PDF is created only when you request export. It may contain your selected photos, property nickname, buyer observations, questions, answers and evidence identifiers. You choose the destination in Android's share interface. Recipient services handle shared copies under their own policies. Nothing is automatically sent to the publisher.

Retention, deletion and backups

Viewing records remain until you use “Delete all local records” in the app or remove the app. Deletion removes the app's viewing archive, evidence and generated report files; it does not erase originals in your photo library, copies you shared, or information held by Google. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for app records. AICore manages its separate model storage. Export important reports before uninstalling or changing phones.

Support and this website

If you email apov@me.com, Alexander Povolotskiy receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. The app does not attach photos, reports or logs automatically. Support correspondence is used only to respond and investigate issues, ordinarily retained no more than 12 months after the last interaction. Request access or deletion by emailing the same address. Remove personal details from optional screenshots.

This website has no advertising, analytics scripts, signup forms or third-party fonts. GitHub Pages may process ordinary hosting logs under GitHub's privacy policy. Google Play may separately process store, testing and platform diagnostics under Google's policies.

No photo training or sale

The app does not upload your viewing photos, notes or reports for model training, sell them, use advertising tracking, or add its own analytics service. Google SDK diagnostic collection is described above. This policy applies to the Android edition; the iOS privacy policy covers the iPhone edition. We will update this policy before materially changing these practices.
