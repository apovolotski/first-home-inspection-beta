Android privacy policy

Alexander Povolotskiy · Updated November 5, 2026

Photos and reports stay on your phone

First Home Inspection helps buyers record a property viewing with a checklist, selected photos, notes, attributed answers and buyer-reviewed observations. There is no account, payment, advertising or cloud photo analysis. We do not request a date of birth. Your photos, selected focus areas, notes, answers, AI suggestions, review decisions and report snapshots are kept in the app's private local storage. The publisher cannot read them unless you deliberately share a report or support message.

Optional experimental photo checks use Qwen3-VL 2B with a llama.cpp runtime bundled in the app. Photos and prompts are processed locally on compatible 64-bit ARM phones with at least 6 GB of RAM. There is no paid API or cloud inference fallback. The checklist, photos and manual reports work without downloading the model.

Camera and selected photos

You choose to open your camera app or the Android system photo picker. The app receives only the photo you capture or select. It does not request unrestricted photo-library access, contacts, precise location, microphone or advertising identifiers. Original selected image files may contain camera metadata, including a location saved by your camera; such metadata remains in the private evidence file. Avoid photographing people, private documents or details you do not want in your viewing record.

Optional model downloads

Preparing local photo analysis downloads about 1.6 GB of public model files from Hugging Face and its delivery providers. The app requests only pinned public model filenames, using a generic app download identifier. A download service receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address, request time and requested public model filename. Its connection logs may include approximate location inferred from your IP address and records of model-download requests. Providers may retain and use these records for delivering files, service analytics and security under their policies. Viewing photos, notes, prompts, reports and contact details are not included. Use Wi-Fi. Interrupted downloads can be resumed. Both model files must match the publisher's SHA-256 hashes before the app enables them.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or Google ML Kit SDK. Model inference uses local files and does not call a cloud AI service. Hosting providers handle their own network logs under their policies. See the Hugging Face privacy policy. The app does not control providers' retention of download logs. These optional connection identifiers, inferred approximate location and download interactions are disclosed separately from your local viewing records in Google Play's Data safety section. You can use the manual app without initiating a model download.

Requests about download logs

For access or erasure requests concerning information held by Hugging Face, contact privacy@huggingface.co and identify your request as a data protection query. Its privacy policy explains the applicable rights and retention limits. Do not send your home photos or reports for such a request. You may also contact our support address for help directing a request. We cannot erase provider logs ourselves or guarantee removal where the provider retains them for security or legal reasons. There is no app account to delete.

You control report sharing

A PDF is created only when you request export. It may contain your selected photos, property nickname, buyer observations, questions, answers and evidence identifiers. You choose the destination in Android's share interface. Recipient services handle shared copies under their own policies. Nothing is automatically sent to the publisher.

Retention, deletion and backups

Viewing records remain until you use “Delete all local records” in the app or remove the app. Deletion removes the app's viewing archive, evidence and generated report files; it does not erase originals in your photo library, copies you shared, or provider connection logs. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for app records and model files. “Remove local model” deletes downloaded model files separately from viewing records; deleting viewing records leaves the model available. Export important reports before uninstalling or changing phones.

Support and this website

If you email apov@me.com, Alexander Povolotskiy receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. The app does not attach photos, reports or logs automatically. Support correspondence is used only to respond and investigate issues, ordinarily retained no more than 12 months after the last interaction. Request access or deletion by emailing the same address. Remove personal details from optional screenshots.

This website has no advertising, analytics scripts, signup forms or third-party fonts. GitHub Pages may process ordinary hosting logs under GitHub's privacy policy. Google Play may separately process store, testing and platform diagnostics under Google's policies.

No photo training or sale

The app does not upload your viewing photos, notes or reports for model training, sell them, use advertising tracking, or add its own analytics service. This policy applies to the Android edition; the iOS privacy policy covers the iPhone edition. We will update this policy before materially changing these practices.
