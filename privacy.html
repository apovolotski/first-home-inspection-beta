Privacy policy

Alexander Povolotskiy · Updated October 13, 2026

Your viewing photos stay on your device

First Home Inspection is a closed beta published by Alexander Povolotskiy. It helps buyers collect viewing photos, notes and seller answers, review possible visible concerns, and export a report. The app does not create an account or upload property photos for AI processing.

Your photos, notes, answers, selected photo areas, model responses, review decisions and report snapshots are saved locally in the app's storage. Optional AI analysis runs on your iPhone. There is no paid API or cloud analysis fallback.

Camera and selected photos

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker gives the app the images you select; the app does not request unrestricted access to your photo library. You can use imported photos or manual reports if camera access is unavailable. Manage camera permission in iOS Settings.

Model downloads and internet access

If you choose to download on-device analysis, model files are fetched from Hugging Face and its delivery providers. These providers receive normal network-request information, such as your IP address and the requested file. Property photos, notes and AI prompts are not included in model downloads. Provider retention is governed by their policies.

After verified model files are installed, photo analysis uses local files and does not need an AI service or API key. Opening the policy or support website contacts GitHub Pages. This site has no analytics scripts, advertising, signup forms or third-party fonts; GitHub may process ordinary hosting logs under its privacy policies.

Sharing a report is your choice

The app exports a PDF only when you request it. A report may contain your photos, property nickname, observations and attributed answers. You choose its destination through the system share interface. Shared copies and their handling are controlled by the recipient or service you choose. Nothing is automatically sent to the publisher.

Apple TestFlight data

When you use this beta through TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and usage information and shares beta diagnostics with the publisher. Email invitations and feedback can include your name and email address. Screenshots and comments you choose to submit are also shared with the publisher. This platform collection is separate from the app's local photo analysis.

The publisher uses beta diagnostics and feedback to investigate defects and improve First Home Inspection, and does not share TestFlight feedback with other parties or use it to train an AI model. Apple's TestFlight privacy information describes Apple's collection and retention. Avoid including addresses, people, private documents or other sensitive details in feedback screenshots.

Support messages

If you email apov@me.com, the publisher receives your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond and investigate the issue. The app does not attach property photos, reports, identifiers or logs automatically. Please remove personal details from any optional screenshots.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local viewing data remains on your device until you delete the home or remove the app. Deleting a home removes its local evidence, report snapshots and the app's temporary PDF exports. It does not delete copies you previously shared, photo-library originals, or older device backups. Downloaded model files are separate from viewing data and are excluded from device backup; saved evidence and reports may be included in your ordinary device backups.

The publisher keeps independently saved support correspondence and beta-feedback copies only as needed for the beta and issue resolution, ordinarily no more than 12 months after the last interaction. You can request access or deletion by emailing apov@me.com. Data retained in Apple's TestFlight systems follows Apple's retention and available deletion controls; the publisher cannot erase backups or recipients' shared copies.

No advertising, tracking or photo-model training

The app includes no advertising or tracking SDK and does not sell viewing data. It does not send your photos to an AI provider or use your viewing photos, notes or reports to train a model. The publisher cannot read locally stored viewings unless you deliberately share them through a report, email or feedback screenshot.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact Alexander Povolotskiy at apov@me.com. This policy describes the current closed beta. If the app's data practices change, the policy and in-app information will be updated before those practices take effect.

Provider information: Apple TestFlight privacy, Hugging Face privacy and GitHub privacy.
